Injects the loop-aware audio player into the web client. After changing this, hard-refresh open browser tabs.
For loop tracks that stop right at the loop end: with Repeat off or Repeat all, play the loop a few times, then fade out and move on. With Repeat One they loop forever as usual. Needs the player above to be enabled.
Tracks with an outro or their own ending after the loop also play the loop the number of times above and fade out, instead of playing their outro.
Adds the disc name from the files' tags (DISCSUBTITLE, or TSST in MP3) next to "Disc 2" etc. Needs the player above to be enabled.
On albums with more than one disc, buttons at the right of each "Disc N" header play or shuffle just that disc. Needs the player above to be enabled.
Marks tracks that have loop tags in album and playlist track lists. Needs the player above to be enabled.
After each library scan, albums get a tag per platform from their files' PLATFORM tag (e.g. "Nintendo 3DS"), so you can filter music by platform under Filter → Tags. Works on its own, without the player.
